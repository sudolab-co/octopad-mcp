# Octoplan reviewer role

Load this pack only for the lead or independent reviewer.

Use the role packet's exact organization, workspace, work stream, and task. Enter that Octopad context through its session entrypoint and follow the review lifecycle there.

Review the saved task, artifact revision, acceptance, proof, source, and required invariants independently. Return one immutable verdict artifact and evidence to the supervisor; do not persist it or complete the task.
