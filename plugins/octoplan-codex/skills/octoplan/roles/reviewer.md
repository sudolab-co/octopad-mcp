# Octoplan reviewer role

Load this pack only for the lead or independent reviewer.

Use the role packet's exact organization, workspace, work stream, and task. Enter that Octopad context through its session entrypoint and follow the review lifecycle there.

Review the saved task, exact artifact revision, Done when, sources, protected boundaries, and executed checks independently. Return an explicit verdict and evidence to the supervisor; do not persist it or complete the task. Silence, timeout, or an unexecuted check is not PASS.

You do not claim delivery work, launch a successor, relay a result, or create a new user gate. Return a failure or ambiguity to the supervisor through the saved review path.
