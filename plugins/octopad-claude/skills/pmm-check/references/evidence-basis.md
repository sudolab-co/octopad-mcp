# Method provenance and further reading

These links were inherited from an earlier internal method on 2026-09-22. They were not fetched or freshly verified in this experiment. They are reading suggestions, not findings established by this file. Verify the source, context, date and exact conclusion before using it to substantiate a research or legal statement. The operational method in the skill is self-contained and makes no empirical conversion or compliance guarantee.

## Keep different kinds of authority separate

The scoped factual owner supplies offer-specific evidence. Competent decision authority accepts positioning choices. Practitioner methods help develop options. Research can inform general hypotheses after assessment; it does not establish what a particular offer does. Official sources require jurisdiction and intended-use analysis by the appropriate specialist before a legal conclusion. None grants access, acceptance or publication authority.

## Research links

- Packard and Berger (2021), *How Concrete Language Shapes Customer Satisfaction*, Journal of Consumer Research: [publisher page](https://academic.oup.com/jcr/article/47/5/787/5873524). Potential reading for concrete language; do not infer a conversion guarantee.
- Amershi and colleagues (2019), *Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction*, CHI: [authors' publication page](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction/). Potential reading for communicating system behavior and controls.
- Lee and See (2004), *Trust in Automation*, Human Factors: [publisher page](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1518/hfes.46.1.50_30392). Potential reading for reliance and trust language.
- Buçinca, Malaya and Gajos (2021), *To Trust or to Think*, CSCW: [publisher page](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3449287). Potential reading for AI decision support and reliance.
- Bansal and colleagues (2021), *Does the Whole Exceed its Parts?*, CHI: [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.14779). Potential reading for explanations and human-AI decisions.

## Official source links

- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework): potential risk-review reference; not a certification for any offer.
- [FTC advertising substantiation policy statement](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/ftc-policy-statement-regarding-advertising-substantiation): source for specialist assessment of applicable advertising rules, not a universal legal conclusion.
- [EU AI Act official text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj): source for specialist assessment of intended use, applicable rules and dates. The skill's sensitive-use tripwires are escalation heuristics, not legal classifications.
